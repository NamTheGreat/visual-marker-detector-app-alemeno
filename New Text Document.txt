### 🔧 **Detection Algorithm**
The app uses a sophisticated **6-stage marker detection pipeline**:
1. **Grayscale Conversion** - RGB to 8-bit luminance
2. **Adaptive Thresholding** - Handles variable lighting
3. **Contour Detection** - Flood-fill connected components
4. **Shape Validation** - Square aspect ratio verification
5. **Multi-Level Marker Validation**:
   - ✅ Border thickness (≈20px)
   - ✅ Corner square (20x20px, top-left)
   - ✅ White space percentage (>60%)
   - ✅ Circularity scoring
6. **Rotation Calculation** - Angle from corner square position
